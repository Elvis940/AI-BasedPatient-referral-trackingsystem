About J.J. Dossen Memorial Hospital

J.J. Dossen Memorial Hospital is a premier healthcare institution committed to providing exceptional medical care to our community. With our new AI-based digital referral and patient tracking system, we're transforming how patients navigate through our healthcare network.

Our innovative system ensures that every patient receives timely, appropriate care by eliminating lost referrals, reducing wait times, and providing real-time tracking throughout their healthcare journey.
